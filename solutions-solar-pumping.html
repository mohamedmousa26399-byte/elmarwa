Solar water pumping

Solar water-pumping systems

Every component a solar pumping system needs: modules, pump drives, structures, cables and protection.

Overview

Solar pumping is where Elmarwa started in solar. We installed 120 solar water-pumping systems in 2016 and, since 2018, supply every component required for solar pumping systems.

Solar pumping replaces diesel with free energy from the sun, cutting fuel and maintenance costs for farms and remote sites.

Typical projects

  • Agricultural irrigation
  • Livestock watering
  • Remote water supply
  • Replacing diesel pumps
