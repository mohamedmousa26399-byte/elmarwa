Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our partners, services and quotations.

Which brands does Elmarwa supply?

We are the authorized distributor of Jinko Solar and Sigenergy in Egypt, a Huawei FusionSolar Gold Partner, a certified distributor of Newmax, and a certified agent for INVT, H.I.S. cables and Suntree. We also supply our own steel and aluminium mounting structures. See all partners.

Can I combine products from different brands in one order?

Yes. You can order Jinko Solar modules, a Huawei or Sigenergy inverter, mounting structures, H.I.S. or Suntree cables and Suntree protection in a single quotation.

Do you cover all of Egypt?

Yes. A wide network of authorized partners and resellers covers the whole Egyptian market geographically.

Do you offer after-sales service and maintenance?

Yes. We provide after-sales technical support and hardware maintenance, on-call and on-site support, and a certified maintenance center for all devices sold by Elmarwa. See our services.

Can you help design my system?

Yes. We offer engineering consultancy and PV system design, and can suggest suitable components for your project.

How do I request a quotation?

Use the quote form, email us, or send your product list on WhatsApp. Include models, quantities and your delivery location if you know them.

What is Solar Valley?

Solar Valley Renewable Energy is Elmarwa's sister company and executive arm in the United Arab Emirates, supplying equipment to our partners across the MENA region. Visit solarvalleypv.com.

Where is Elmarwa located?

Our office is at 70 El Mariotya Street, Nile Towers, Faisal, Giza, Egypt. Open in Google Maps.
