About the brand

Jinko Solar at Elmarwa

JinkoSolar is one of the world's most recognized solar technology companies. Its products serve more than 4,000 customers in nearly 200 countries, and by the end of 2025 its cumulative shipments had exceeded 350 GW.

Elmarwa is the authorized distributor of Jinko Solar in Egypt. We were the only distributor to receive Jinko Solar's Gold Partner certificate in 2021, earned its Platinum Partner certificate in 2022, and were recognized as its top-performing PV module distributor in the Middle East.
