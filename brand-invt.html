About the brand

INVT at Elmarwa

Founded in 2002, INVT focuses on industrial automation and energy power. It went public in 2010 and today has 15 subsidiaries, more than 3,200 employees and a sales network covering more than 90 countries and regions.

INVT is a Key High-tech Enterprise of China's National Torch Plan, recognized for its technology in power electronics and automation control. Elmarwa is a certified agent for INVT.
