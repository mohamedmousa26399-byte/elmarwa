About the brand

Sigenergy at Elmarwa

Sigenergy builds intelligent solar-plus-storage systems. For homes and residential projects, SigenStor Neo combines a Neo hybrid inverter with stackable Neo batteries, adding backup power, smart load management and EV charging, all managed through the mySigen app.

For commercial and industrial sites, SigenStack is a hybrid solution made up of Sigen HYB inverters (50–125 kW), modular SigenStack batteries and the Sigen Gateway. Elmarwa is the authorized distributor of Sigenergy in Egypt.
