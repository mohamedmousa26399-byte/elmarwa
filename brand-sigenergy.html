About the brand

Sigenergy at Elmarwa

Sigenergy's home energy solution is an intelligent residential energy ecosystem that combines solar PV, battery storage, backup power, smart load management and EV charging on one platform.

Powered by Sigen AI, it analyzes household demand, solar generation, weather and electricity tariffs to optimize energy flow and increase solar self-consumption, with monitoring and control through the mySigen app. Elmarwa is the authorized distributor of Sigenergy in Egypt.
