About the brand

Huawei FusionSolar at Elmarwa

Huawei is a global leader in smart PV solutions, with more than 30 years of expertise in digital information technology. Its inverters bring ICT technology into PV systems, making solar plants efficient, safe and reliable, with smart O&M and grid-support capabilities.

Elmarwa became a Huawei FusionSolar inverter agent in 2021, a Silver Partner in 2022 and a Gold Partner in 2023. In 2025 we supplied 85 MW of on-grid inverters.
