We supply storage from Huawei FusionSolar and Sigenergy, designed to pair with their own inverters for an integrated, easy-to-monitor system.

Solar pumping inverters

Brands we supply
INVT

INVT solar pumping inverters drive AC water pumps directly from PV panels, with no grid connection or diesel generator needed.

They are a cost-effective solution for agriculture, irrigation, livestock watering and water supply at remote locations.

Typical applications

  • Agricultural irrigation
  • Farm and livestock water supply
  • Off-grid and remote sites
  • Replacing diesel-powered pumps

DC protection

Brands we supply
Suntree

A safe PV system needs proper protection on the DC side. Suntree specialises in DC protection devices for solar installations.

We supply the components you need to protect strings, inverters and people, and to meet installation and inspection requirements.

  • DC fuses and fuse holders
