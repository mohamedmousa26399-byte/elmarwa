Solar pump drives

Brands we supply
INVT

Solar pumping is where Elmarwa started in solar: we installed 120 solar water-pumping systems in 2016, and since 2018 we supply every component a pumping system needs.

We supply INVT solar pump drives, which run standard AC pumps directly from a PV array, with no grid connection or diesel generator needed.

Typical applications

  • Agricultural irrigation
  • Farm and livestock water supply
  • Remote and off-grid sites
  • Replacing diesel-powered pumps

Mounting structures

Supplied by
Elmarwa

Elmarwa supplies mounting structures in hot-dip galvanized steel and in aluminium. Zinc coating protects steel against corrosion, and its service life grows with the coating thickness and suits the local climate.

Our aluminium structures use 6063 aluminium alloy, which resists corrosion and holds up in extreme weather.

Aluminium structure specifications

Alloy6063 aluminium alloy: corrosion-resistant, less brittle and malleable. DIN alloy certificate available on request.
Material thicknessTriangle beams 1.8–2.1 mm, lateral beams 1.4–1.7 mm, flush mounting structure 1.6–1.9 mm
Design loadWind speed of 130 km/h, with 5-second gusts
Warranty25 years from Elmarwa

Typical applications

  • Ground-mounted plants
  • Rooftop systems
  • Solar pumping arrays
  • Flush-mounted installations
