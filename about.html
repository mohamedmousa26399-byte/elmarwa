About Elmarwa

Fulfill Your Ambitions. From an import and export company in 2008 to the highest-selling player in Egypt's solar sector.

Who we are

Egypt's trusted solar distributor

Elmarwa Trade & Supply & Renewable Energy was founded in 2008 as an import and export company and entered the solar energy sector in 2013.

Today we are an authorized distributor, certified agent or partner of seven global brands, including Jinko Solar, Huawei FusionSolar and Sigenergy. In 2025 we recorded total sales of 310 MW, plus 85 MW of on-grid inverters.

A wide network of authorized partners and resellers covers the whole Egyptian market, and our sister company Solar Valley serves partners across the MENA region from the UAE.

History in steps

From 2008 to today

Key milestones in Elmarwa's growth.

  1. 2008

    Founded as an import and export company.

  2. 2013

    Entered the solar energy sector.

  3. 2016

    Installed 120 solar water-pumping systems. Became an authorized distributor of Jinko Solar PV modules and an EPC equipment supplier.

  4. 2018

    Became a complete solution provider, delivering every component required for solar pumping systems.

  5. 2020

    Supplied about 56 MWp of PV modules, bringing cumulative volume with Jinko Solar to 100 MWp.

  6. 2021

    Supplied about 110 MWp of PV modules. Became the only distributor to receive Jinko Solar's Gold Partner certificate, and became a Huawei FusionSolar inverter agent.

  7. 2022

    Became a Huawei FusionSolar Silver Partner and supplied more than 40 MW of on-grid inverters. Earned Jinko Solar's Platinum Partner certificate and was recognized as its top-performing PV module distributor in the Middle East.

  8. 2023

    Supplied about 100 MWp of PV modules and became a Huawei FusionSolar Gold Partner.

  9. 2024

    Recorded total sales of 150 MW, becoming the top-selling provider in the Egyptian solar market.

  10. 2025

    Recorded total sales of 310 MW, plus 85 MW of on-grid inverters, establishing Elmarwa as the highest-selling player in Egypt's solar sector.
