About Elmarwa

A UAE-based trading company bringing leading solar brands together in one place.

Who we are

A multi-brand solar supplier

Elmarwa Trade & Supply & Renewable Energy is a trading company based in the United Arab Emirates, specialising in components for solar power systems.

Rather than tying our customers to a single manufacturer, we work with specialist brands for each part of the system: Jinko Solar for modules, Huawei FusionSolar and Sigenergy for inverters and storage, INVT for solar pumping, and HIS and Suntree for DC cables and protection.

Our goal is simple: the right components for each project, clear quotations and reliable delivery.
